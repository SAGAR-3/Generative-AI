SECTION 1: FHA LOAN PROGRAM

1.1 Program Overview
The Federal Housing Administration (FHA) loan program is designed to help
creditworthy low-to-moderate income borrowers purchase homes with a lower
down payment requirement than conventional loans. FHA loans are insured by
the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD).

1.2 Minimum Eligibility Requirements

Credit Score Requirements:
- 580 or above: Eligible for 3.5% minimum down payment
- 500 to 579: Eligible with 10% minimum down payment
- Below 500: Not eligible for FHA-insured financing

Debt-to-Income (DTI) Ratios:
- Maximum front-end (housing) DTI: 31% of gross monthly income
- Maximum back-end (total debt) DTI: 43% standard
- Up to 50% DTI with AUS approval and compensating factors

Employment Requirements:
- Minimum 2-year employment history required
- Self-employed: 2 years tax returns and YTD profit/loss statement
- Recent job changes acceptable if in same field or improved income

1.3 Required Documentation Checklist
□ Last 2 years W-2 forms
□ Last 2 years signed federal tax returns
□ Most recent 30-day pay stubs
□ Last 3 months bank statements (all pages)
□ Government-issued photo ID
□ Social Security number
□ Purchase contract (if applicable)
□ Property address and legal description
□ Landlord information for rental history

1.4 FHA Mortgage Insurance Premiums (MIP)
All FHA loans require two types of mortgage insurance:
- Upfront MIP (UFMIP): 1.75% of base loan amount, financed into loan
- Annual MIP: 0.55% for 30-year loans with LTV > 90% (varies by term/LTV)

MIP Duration:
- Down payment ≥ 10%: MIP for 11 years
- Down payment < 10%: MIP for life of loan (unless refinanced)

SECTION 2: CONVENTIONAL LOAN PROGRAM

2.1 Conforming Loan Limits
Conforming loan limits are set annually by FHFA. For 2024:
- Single-family home: $766,550 (standard)
- High-cost areas: Up to $1,149,825
- Loans above these limits are classified as Jumbo loans

2.2 Eligibility Criteria

Credit Score Requirements:
- Minimum 620 FICO score for most programs
- Scores 720+ qualify for best available rates
- Scores below 660 may require additional documentation

Down Payment Requirements:
- Minimum 3% down for first-time homebuyers (HomeReady/Home Possible)
- Minimum 5% down for standard conventional
- Minimum 20% down to avoid Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)

DTI Limits:
- Standard maximum: 45% total DTI
- With compensating factors + AUS: Up to 50% total DTI

2.3 Private Mortgage Insurance (PMI)
PMI is required when LTV exceeds 80%:
- Monthly premium: 0.20% - 2.00% annually based on credit/LTV
- Borrower-paid PMI can be canceled when LTV reaches 80% (Homeowners
  Protection Act requires automatic cancellation at 78% LTV)
- Lender-paid PMI option available at slightly higher interest rate

SECTION 3: VA LOAN PROGRAM

3.1 Eligibility Requirements
VA loans are available to:
- Active duty service members (90+ days during wartime, 181+ peacetime)
- Veterans with honorable or general discharge
- National Guard/Reserve members (6+ years service)
- Surviving spouses of service members killed in action

3.2 Key Benefits
- No down payment required in most cases
- No PMI/MIP requirement
- Competitive interest rates
- Limited closing costs
- VA funding fee: 2.15% first use, 3.30% subsequent (waived for disabled veterans)

3.3 Credit and Income Requirements
- No official minimum credit score (lender overlays typically 620+)
- Residual income requirement must be met (varies by family size and region)
- Maximum DTI: 41% guideline (exceptions permitted with residual income)

SECTION 4: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

4.1 RESPA (Real Estate Settlement Procedures Act) Requirements
Under Regulation X (12 CFR Part 1024):

Loan Estimate (LE):
- Must be provided within 3 business days of complete application
- Complete application defined as: borrower name, income, SSN, property address,
  estimated value, and loan amount requested
- Tolerance buckets apply to fees listed on LE

Closing Disclosure (CD):
- Must be provided 3 business days before consummation
- Ensures borrowers can review final terms before closing

Anti-Kickback (Section 8):
- Prohibits payment of referral fees for settlement services
- All title, appraisal, and settlement service providers must be independent

4.2 TILA (Truth in Lending Act) Requirements
Under Regulation Z (12 CFR Part 1026):

Required Disclosures:
- Annual Percentage Rate (APR) — must include all finance charges
- Total amount financed
- Total payments over life of loan
- Payment schedule

Right of Rescission:
- Applies to refinances of primary residence (not purchase)
- 3 business day rescission period

4.3 ECOA (Equal Credit Opportunity Act) Requirements
Under Regulation B (12 CFR Part 1002):

Prohibited Discrimination Bases:
- Race, color, religion, national origin
- Sex, marital status, age
- Receipt of public assistance income
- Exercise of rights under Consumer Credit Protection Act

Adverse Action Notices:
- Required within 30 days of application decision
- Must state specific reasons for denial
- Must include ECOA notice and credit score disclosure if applicable

4.4 FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act) Requirements
- Must obtain written authorization before pulling credit reports
- Adverse action notice required with credit score range
- Consumers have right to dispute inaccurate information
- Credit reports must be kept confidential

SECTION 5: LOAN PROCESS OVERVIEW

5.1 Standard Application Timeline

Day 0:    Application received
Day 1-3:  Loan Estimate issued (within 3 business days)
Day 1-7:  Initial processing; document collection
Day 7-14: Appraisal ordered and completed
Day 14-21: Underwriting review
Day 21-25: Conditional approval issued; conditions cleared
Day 25-27: Closing Disclosure issued (3 days before closing)
Day 30:   Closing/Consummation

5.2 Common Underwriting Conditions
- Verification of employment (VOE) - verbal VOE day of closing
- Updated bank statements (if >60 days old)
- Explanation letters for derogatory credit events
- Gift letter if down payment is gifted
- Homeowner's insurance binder

5.3 Rate Lock Policy
- Standard rate locks: 30, 45, 60 days
- Extended locks available for new construction
- Rate lock fee may apply for locks > 60 days
- Float-down option available (contact rate desk)
- Rates quoted are subject to change; not a commitment to lend

SECTION 6: FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Can I use gift funds for my down payment?
A: Yes. For FHA loans, 100% of the down payment can come from a gift from
a family member. For conventional loans, gift funds are allowed but donor
must provide a gift letter stating no repayment is expected.

Q: What is the difference between pre-qualification and pre-approval?
A: Pre-qualification is an informal estimate based on self-reported information.
Pre-approval involves verified documentation review and a credit pull, providing
a conditional commitment for a specific loan amount.

Q: How long does negative credit history affect my application?
A: Most derogatory items (late payments, collections) remain on credit reports
for 7 years. Bankruptcies: Chapter 7 = 7 years (FHA) / 4 years (conventional).
Chapter 13 = 2 years from discharge. Foreclosure: 3 years (FHA) / 7 years (conventional).

Q: What is the maximum seller concession allowed?
A: FHA: Up to 6% of the purchase price. Conventional: 3% (LTV > 90%),
6% (LTV 75-90%), 9% (LTV ≤ 75%). VA: Up to 4% for certain concessions.
